## Accepted Contract Checklist

- Worker resources exclude `solve.py`, `verify.py`, `recovery_tool.py`,
  precomputed `outputs/`, and scratch worktrees.
- Prompt uses Objective, Provided files, Deliverables, Success criteria, and
  Constraints sections.
- Recovery runs offline after ZIP extraction.
- `repair_log.orphaned_shas` preserves one 7-character short SHA per leading
  reflog SHA in first-seen order.
- Repaired bundles clone and pass `git fsck --connectivity-only`.
- Exact refs, topology, and required Git blob IDs match independent fixtures.
- Submitted `outputs/recovery_tool.py` restores the partial-overlap case.
- Submitted `outputs/recovery_tool.py` rejects the corrupted bundle with
  `error="after_bundle_invalid"` and emits no repaired bundle.
- Determinism checks rerun the submitted tool and do not depend on reference
  `solve.py`.
- Allowed verifier reason codes are `MISSING_FILE`, `SCHEMA_INVALID`,
  `TEST_FAIL`, `CONTRACT_DRIFT`, `NON_DETERMINISTIC_OUTPUT`, and
  `INVALID_CASE_ACCEPTED`.
